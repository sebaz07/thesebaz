Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TheSebaz Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the TheSebaz channel to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

Data the app uses

The app only accesses the owner's own accounts, through the official Google (YouTube Data API) and Meta (Graph API) authorization flows, and only to upload videos and read the result of each upload.

Data storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data, and it does not access data of any other person.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in the Facebook settings under "Business integrations".
